Ph.D. student · WHU × SII

Kang He 何慷

I am a Ph.D. student at Wuhan University and the Shanghai Innovation Institute (SII). My advisors are Donghong Ji, Xipeng Qiu, and Kaipeng Zhang.

I build video datasets for interactive world models. I also study how models interpret sentiment and stance across modalities, and how language agents reason through tasks that take many steps.

I received my M.S. and B.S. degrees from Wuhan University, where I worked with Prof. Donghong Ji and Prof. Mang Ye, respectively.

Research interests

World modeling

Video datasets, camera-conditioned generation, and interactive world models that sustain coherent exploration over time.

GameVerse · Sekai2 · AlayaWorld

Multimodal understanding

Sentiment and stance understanding across language, vision, and audio, with a focus on robust learning under missing or imbalanced modalities.

MMDS-Bench · EASE · EBMC · PaSE

LLM reasoning & agents

Benchmarks for reasoning, scientific-paper understanding, and long-horizon agentic programming.

LongCLI-Bench · PaperGraph-Bench
